Hangeul Cubs — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

Hangeul Cubs is a Korean-learning app by A.P LocaLift (A.P Holdings). We designed it to collect nothing.

The short version

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. The app makes no network requests at all — every lesson, image, and sound works entirely offline on your device.

What the app stores

Nothing is written to your device's storage. Lesson progress, scores, and streaks live only in memory while the app is open.

Audio

Pronunciation uses your device's built-in Korean text-to-speech voice. No audio is recorded, and nothing leaves the device.

Third-party services

None. No SDKs, no trackers, no analytics, no ads.

Children

The app is suitable for learners of all ages. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children — because it collects no data at all.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
